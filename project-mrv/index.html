Project MRV · For project developers

Monitoring data a verifier will accept, from field to report.

Carbon and nature projects live or die on baseline and monitoring data. Climoz builds MRV systems on Zoho that take field records, imagery and calculations through to a verification pack.

Book a 30-minute data review

What the system does

  • Project registry. Sites, parcels, participants and baselines in one place.
  • Offline field app. Monitoring visits with GPS, photos and voice notes, synced when back in signal.
  • Remote data imports. Satellite and drone data brought in alongside field records.
  • Calculations with method. Growth, removals or reductions calculated with the factor and version on the page.
  • Review steps. Field data checked and approved before it reaches a report.
  • Verification pack. Records arranged for your verifier and registry format.
What you get

A verification pack with a trail behind it

Every number in the monitoring report traces back to a visit, a photo, a reading or an import.

Output: CCTS or voluntary-registry formats

Yes. Field teams can record visits offline and sync when they reconnect.
