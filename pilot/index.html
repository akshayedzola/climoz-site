Fixed-scope pilot

Start with one process and one fixed price.

A pilot builds one working system for one real problem, such as getting CBAM data in from the suppliers of one product line. The scope, price and success criteria are agreed in writing before we start.

Book a 30-minute data review

Fixed-scope pilots from £5,000 / USD 6,500. Price confirmed in writing after the 2-week diagnostic.

What every pilot includes

  • A written scope. One process, the sites and suppliers it covers, and the outputs it must produce.
  • A fixed price. Agreed after the diagnostic. Changes are agreed in writing, with their price, before we do them.
  • Success criteria up front. Three to five checks, written by you and us, that decide whether the pilot worked.
  • Your real data. We load a real sample, not demo records, so the result tells you something.
  • Your Zoho account. The system is built where you will run it, and stays there.
  • Handover. Trained users and short admin notes, so your team can run it without us.
Example

CBAM readiness pilot

Scope: one installation or product group, a supplier data request for its named precursor suppliers, an embedded-emissions calculation using the method you choose, and a verifier-ready export.

Success criteria, for example: every named supplier has a portal login; the system reproduces a shipment you have already worked out by hand; your team can run a quarter's figures without us.

Not included: verification, legal advice, or submitting declarations to the authorities.

Questions

Common questions

How much does a pilot cost?

Fixed-scope pilots start from £5,000 / USD 6,500. The exact price depends on the scope agreed in the diagnostic, and you get it in writing before any build starts.

Are we committed to a full build afterwards?

No. Before the pilot ends we quote the next phase as a fixed price. You decide whether to go ahead.

What happens to the pilot system if we stop?

It stays in your Zoho account, with your data, and it keeps working. You can run it yourself.

Will you publish a case study about us?

Only with your written permission, and you approve the wording before anything is published.
