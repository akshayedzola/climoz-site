Supplier evidence · For suppliers

Answer EcoVadis, Sedex and customer questionnaires from one set of evidence.

Every rating and every customer asks for the same policies, certificates and figures in a different format. Climoz builds an evidence library on Zoho where each item has an owner, a renewal date and a link to every question it answers.

Book a 30-minute data review

What the system does

  • Evidence library. Policies, certificates, procedures and KPIs, each with an owner, a version and an expiry date.
  • Question mapping. Each item is linked to the EcoVadis themes, Sedex SAQ sections and customer questions it answers.
  • Request tracker. Every incoming questionnaire, its deadline, its status and who is working on it.
  • Renewal alerts. Owners are reminded before a certificate expires or an assessment comes round.
  • Answer bank. Approved answers you can reuse, each with the document behind it.
  • Evidence packs. Export the documents a specific request needs, in one go.

Questions

Common questions

Will Climoz get us an EcoVadis medal?

No. EcoVadis rates your company, and we have no influence over ratings. We make sure the evidence you submit is complete, current and easy to find, which is the part you control.

Do we still need EcoVadis or Sedex?

Yes, if your customers ask for them. Climoz holds the evidence. The schemes and your customers still receive the answers through their own platforms.

Which requests can the library cover?

EcoVadis, Sedex SAQs and audit follow-ups, CDP supply chain requests, modern slavery due diligence, customer codes of conduct, tender questionnaires and CBAM supplier data requests.

How long does it take?

Most evidence libraries are live in 6 to 12 weeks after a 2-week diagnostic, depending on the number of sites and schemes.

Who owns the system?

You do. It is built in your own Zoho account, and you can keep running it with or without us.
