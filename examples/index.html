What every pattern has in common

  • A question first. We design the system around the question someone will ask of it, not around a form.
  • Inputs with their source. Every number links to the invoice, reading, declaration or photo it came from.
  • Method on the page. Factors and formulas are stored with their year and version, so last year's answer can be rebuilt.
  • Flags, not silence. Estimates, missing data and expiring evidence are shown, not smoothed over.
