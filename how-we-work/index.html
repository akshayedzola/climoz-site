How we work

From a 30-minute call to a working system in about three months.

We don't start from zero, and we don't leave until your team is using it.

Book a 30-minute data review
Step 130 min

Data review

We look at what your customers, raters or regulators are asking for and where your data lives today. You leave with a clear next step, whether or not it's us.

Step 22 weeks

Diagnostic

We map sites, suppliers, evidence, calculations and reporting formats, and agree a fixed scope and price.

Step 36–12 weeks

Build

We configure the system from our templates, load your real data and test it with the people who'll use it.

Step 4Ongoing

Support

Factor updates, new reporting cycles and rule changes. The system keeps up as CBAM, ESOS and reporting rules move.

What you get at each step

  • After the review: a short note on what is being asked of you and the smallest system that would help.
  • After the diagnostic: a map of your data, a fixed scope, a fixed price and a timeline.
  • During the build: a working system you can click through every two weeks, with your real data.
  • At go-live: trained users, admin documentation and a system in your own Zoho account.
Working with advisers

Already working with an ESG consultant or verifier?

Good. They advise and sign off; we build the data system they rely on. We are happy to work to their method and give them direct access to the evidence trail.

What we don't do: certify claims, award ratings or promise compliance.

Questions

Common questions

How much does it cost?

Fixed-scope pilots start from £5,000 / USD 6,500. You get a fixed price for a fixed scope after the 2-week diagnostic, and the diagnostic itself is scoped and priced up front.

What if our needs change during the build?

We agree changes in writing, with their effect on price and timeline, before doing them.

Who do we work with?

A small team that builds the system with you: a lead who scopes and tests, and the people who configure it on Zoho.

What happens after go-live?

Ongoing support covers factor updates, new reporting cycles, scheme changes and new users. You can also run the system yourself.
