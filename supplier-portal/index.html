Supplier data portals · For procurement

Supplier ESG data you can score, from a portal suppliers will finish.

Spreadsheet questionnaires come back late, incomplete and in forty formats. Climoz builds a supplier portal on Zoho with your questions, your scoring and reminders that chase for you.

What the system does

  • Your questionnaire. Your own questions with branching, file uploads and save-and-return, usable on a phone.
  • Supplier logins. Each supplier sees only its own records and documents.
  • Code-of-conduct sign-off. Track who has signed which version, and when.
  • Document expiry alerts. Certificates and audits flagged before they lapse.
  • Risk tiers and scores. Your weighting rules turn answers into a tier your buyers can use.
  • Reporting feed. Value-chain data in the shape your sustainability team needs.
What you get

Supplier scores you can act on

Procurement sees who is complete, who is high risk and who needs a nudge. Sustainability gets supplier data it can use in reporting without re-keying it.

Questions

Common questions

How is this different from an ESG platform's supplier module?

Platforms give you fixed forms. Climoz builds the questionnaire, scoring and workflow around your categories, risk rules and existing supplier records.

Do suppliers need to install anything?

No. Suppliers use a web portal in their browser, on a laptop or a phone.

Can it use the supplier data we already hold?

Yes. We load your existing supplier list and records during the build, and can connect to Zoho CRM or import from your ERP exports.

Who owns the data?

You do. The portal runs in your own Zoho account.
