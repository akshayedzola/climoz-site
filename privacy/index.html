Privacy notice

What happens to the information you share with Climoz through this website.

Last updated 2 November 2026

Who we are

Climoz is a brand of EdZola, which operates this website and is responsible for the information you share through it. EdZola is based in Coimbatore, India. EdZola's privacy policy applies to Climoz alongside this notice.

What we collect, and why

When you use the enquiry form we collect your name, company, work email, country, team, the needs you select and your message. We use this only to reply to your request and to follow up about it. You choose whether to send it, and we ask for your agreement before contacting you.

Where it goes

Enquiries are stored in EdZola's Zoho CRM account. Zoho processes this data on EdZola's behalf. We do not sell your information or share it with other companies for their marketing.

Cookies and third parties

  • We do not currently use analytics or advertising cookies on this site.
  • If we add analytics or chat, they will load only after you accept them, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • The enquiry form loads a small script from Zoho to protect against spam submissions.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records for as long as we need them to respond and follow up, in line with EdZola's privacy policy.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information you have sent us, or ask us to stop contacting you. Use the enquiry form or the contact details in EdZola's privacy policy, and tell us it is a privacy request.
