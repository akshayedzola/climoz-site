Questions

Common questions

Is Climoz an assurance provider for BRSR Core?

No. Climoz builds the data system. Independent assessment or assurance is done by a qualified provider, and the system gives them a clear trail.

Which emission factors do you use in India?

The factors your method requires, for example the CEA grid emission factor for the relevant year. Each factor is stored with its year and source.

Where is the data hosted?

In your own Zoho account. Zoho runs data centres in India if you need data to stay in the country.
