BRSR Core and value chain data: what to collect, from whom, and how to prove it

BRSR Core assessment or assurance reaches the top 1,000 listed companies in FY 2026-27. Value chain disclosures are voluntary for now, but the data takes a year to set up. This guide covers the rules as SEBI last set them, and the operating model that makes the figures defensible.

The short answer

This guide covers India only. BRSR Core is a subset of BRSR: key indicators under 9 ESG attributes that must be independently assessed or assured. Under SEBI's circular of 28 March 2025, that applies to the top 1,000 listed entities by market capitalisation from FY 2026-27. Value chain disclosures apply to the top 250 on a voluntary basis from FY 2025-26, and assessment or assurance of them is voluntary from FY 2026-27.

BRSR Core: who and when

SEBI's March 2025 circular kept the original phase-in, but let companies choose between assessment (a third-party assessment under standards developed by the Industry Standards Forum, in consultation with SEBI) and assurance.

Financial yearBRSR Core assessment or assurance applies to
2023-24Top 150 listed entities
2024-25Top 250 listed entities
2025-26Top 500 listed entities
2026-27Top 1,000 listed entities

By market capitalisation. Source: SEBI circular SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2025/42, 28 March 2025, para 3.6.

The provider must have the necessary expertise and no conflict of interest. SEBI's example: the provider and its associates must not sell products or provide consulting or other non-assurance services to the company or its group. Plan this early if your current ESG consultant was hoping to do the assessment as well.

The circular also added a leadership indicator under Principle 6 on green credits generated or procured, by the company and by its top ten value chain partners, from FY 2024-25.

Value chain: which partners count

For BRSR Core purposes, the value chain is the company's top upstream and downstream partners that each account for 2% or more of its purchases or sales by value. The company may limit disclosure to partners covering 75% of its purchases and sales, respectively.

Disclosure is voluntary for the top 250 from FY 2025-26. That was a one-year deferral and a looser threshold compared with the original 2023 framework. Voluntary does not mean ignored: large buyers are already asking their main suppliers for BRSR Core-style figures to get ready.

Illustrative figures. Nine suppliers are at or above 2%. The company may stop at Supplier H, the point where cumulative coverage reaches 75%, so it collects data from 8 suppliers instead of 9. Run the same exercise for sales.

Recalculate this list every year from the finance ledger, not from memory. Store the ranking, the cut-off and the date with the disclosure, so your assessor can see how the partner list was chosen.

Owners, approvals and evidence

Assessors test whether a figure can be traced to its source and whether someone accountable approved it. For each BRSR Core indicator, set up four things:

Owner and approver roles are a suggested starting point. Use the exact indicator list in the BRSR Core format (Annexure 17A of the SEBI master circular) for your year.

  • One named owner per indicator per site. Not a department. A person, with a backup.
  • Evidence attached at entry. Uploading the bill when the figure is entered is far cheaper than finding it in March.
  • A review step with comments. The assessor will ask what changed and why, and the comment trail answers that.
  • Versioned factors and methods. Store the emission factor's year and source, for example the CEA grid factor, so last year's figure can be rebuilt.
  • Value chain partners treated as data owners too. Give them their own login, a short form and a deadline, instead of an email with a spreadsheet.
