Supplier evidence · Guide

Answer customer sustainability questionnaires once

EcoVadis this month, a Sedex SAQ next month, then three customers with their own spreadsheets. They look different, but they ask for much the same evidence. Here is where they overlap and what to do about it.

Published 2 November 2026 · Climoz

The requests suppliers get

  • EcoVadis. A rating across environment, labour and human rights, ethics and sustainable procurement, renewed every 12 months.
  • Sedex SAQ. A self-assessment questionnaire on labour, health and safety, environment and business ethics, often followed by a SMETA audit.
  • CDP supply chain. Customers who are CDP supply chain members ask suppliers to disclose on climate and, in some cases, water and forests.
  • Customer ESG questionnaires. Each customer's own list, usually a spreadsheet or a supplier portal, often mirroring the schemes above.
  • CBAM data requests. EU importers, and UK importers from 2027, ask producers for embedded emissions of specific goods.
  • Modern slavery due diligence. UK customers covered by the Modern Slavery Act ask suppliers how they manage forced-labour risk.

Where they overlap

Map each piece of evidence against the requests it answers, and the overlap is hard to miss. Most requests draw on the same dozen or so policies, certificates and KPIs.

What a 50 to 500 person supplier can do this quarter

None of this needs new software to start. It needs a list and some discipline.

  1. List every request from the last 12 months. Scheme, customer, deadline and who answered it.
  2. Build one master evidence list. Every document and figure those requests asked for, de-duplicated.
  3. Give each item an owner and a renewal date. One named person, one date.
  4. Write an answer bank. Approved answers to the questions that come up again and again, each linked to its evidence.
  5. Fix the gaps that appear most often. If five requests ask for a GHG inventory and you don't have one, that is your priority.
  6. Decide where it lives. A disciplined shared drive works for a while. When requests pile up, a system with owners, alerts and mapping pays for itself.

Start with the request that's due first. If an EcoVadis assessment is coming, our EcoVadis evidence checklist covers most of the master list.
