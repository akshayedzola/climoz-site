EcoVadis evidence checklist: what to collect before your assessment

A customer has asked for your EcoVadis scorecard. The questionnaire is the visible part. Most of the work is finding current, signed evidence for every answer. This checklist lists what to gather, theme by theme.

What EcoVadis is, and isn't

EcoVadis is a sustainability rating, not a certification. Your company answers a questionnaire tailored to its industry, size and countries of operation, and uploads documents to support the answers. EcoVadis analysts then score your management system across four themes: Environment, Labour and Human Rights, Ethics and Sustainable Procurement.

The result is a scorecard out of 100, valid for 12 months. Depending on how your score compares with other rated companies, you may also receive a medal or badge.

Customers usually ask for it because their own procurement policy, supplier scorecards or sustainability reporting depend on supplier ratings. That is why the request tends to come back every year.

How evidence is used

EcoVadis looks for three things in each theme: policies (what you commit to), actions (what you do about it) and results (what you measure and report). Claims without supporting documents typically carry little weight. A dated, signed policy and a year of KPIs usually count for more than a polished paragraph.

The common failure: last year's upload is reused, half the certificates have expired, and nobody knows who owns the renewals. Give every evidence item an owner and a renewal date, and the reassessment becomes an update rather than a hunt.

How to prepare

  1. Start from last year's scorecard. Its improvement areas tell you where evidence was missing or weak.
  2. Give every item an owner. One named person per document, not a department.
  3. Date and sign policies. Undated or unsigned documents are harder to rely on.
  4. Prefer results to intentions. A year of incident or energy data says more than a commitment to measure it.
  5. Track renewals. Certificates, permits and audits each have their own cycle. Put the dates somewhere everyone can see.
  6. Keep the final upload. Store exactly what you submitted, so next year starts from it.

Reuse the same evidence elsewhere

Most of this list also answers Sedex SAQ sections, CDP supply chain requests and your customers' own sustainability questionnaires. Holding it once, with owners and renewal dates, means each new request starts from evidence you already have. Questions

Is EcoVadis a certification?

No. EcoVadis is a sustainability rating. It scores your company out of 100 across four themes, and may award a medal or badge based on how your score compares with other rated companies. It does not certify that you comply with any law or standard.

How long is an EcoVadis scorecard valid?

A scorecard is valid for 12 months, so most companies are reassessed every year.

How early should we start collecting evidence?

If the evidence already lives in one place with owners, a few weeks is enough. If it is scattered, start two to three months before the questionnaire is due.

Does the same evidence work for Sedex and customer questionnaires?

Largely, yes. Most of the policies, certificates and KPIs above also answer Sedex SAQ sections and customer sustainability questionnaires.

What does EcoVadis cost?

Fees are set by EcoVadis and depend on your company size and the subscription level you choose. Check the current price list with EcoVadis directly.
