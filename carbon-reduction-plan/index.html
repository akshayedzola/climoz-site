What the system does

  • Activity data by site. Fuel, electricity, travel, waste and transport entries, each with its evidence.
  • Factors versioned by year. Conversion factors stored by year and source, so last year's total stays reproducible.
  • Scope 1, 2 and 3. A complete Scope 1 and 2 inventory and a practical start on the Scope 3 categories you report.
  • Baseline tracking. Your baseline year and progress against it, recalculated when your organisation changes.
  • Plan output. Figures and reduction measures laid out in the structure your plan needs.
  • SECR and ESOS data. Energy and emissions figures reused for directors' reports and ESOS.

Questions

Common questions

Do you write the carbon reduction plan?

We produce the figures and a draft in the structure the plan needs. Your organisation sets the commitments, your board approves it and you publish it.

Which emission factors do you use?

Whatever your method requires, for example the UK Government conversion factors for the relevant year. Each factor is stored with its year and source.

Can we start with Scope 1 and 2 only?

Yes. Most clients start with a solid Scope 1 and 2 inventory and add Scope 3 categories as data improves.

Do we still need a consultant?

Sometimes. A consultant can advise on targets and method. Climoz builds the system that holds the data and produces the figures.
