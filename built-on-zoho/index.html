Questions

Common questions

Do we need Zoho licences?

Yes. The system runs in your own Zoho account, on licences you hold. We help you pick the right plan for your users during the diagnostic.

What if we stop working with Climoz?

You keep the system and the data. It is in your account, and another Zoho partner or your own team can maintain it.

Can the data stay in our region?

Zoho runs data centres in the EU, US, Australia, Canada, Saudi Arabia, UAE and India. Your account is set up in the region you choose.

Can it connect to our other systems?

Usually, yes. Zoho has APIs and connectors, and we can import from ERP, HR or finance exports where a live connection isn't needed.
