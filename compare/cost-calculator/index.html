How it works out the totals

For each option: one-off cost, plus for each year the yearly fees and the staff hours per cycle × cycles per year × your cost per hour.

  • Staff hours matter most. For most teams, internal time costs more than any licence. Estimate it honestly from last cycle.
  • Use real quotes. Platform pricing and Zoho licence counts vary by users and modules. Ask for a written quote for the users you will actually have.
  • Run more than one scenario. Try 1, 3 and 5 years, and a year when your supplier count doubles.
Not included

What the sums leave out

Risk is hard to price: a figure you can't trace in a customer audit, a supplier list that lives in one person's inbox, or switching costs when a contract ends. Weigh those alongside the totals.
