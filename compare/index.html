When to pick each

Choose by your situation, not by the feature list

Spreadsheets

Pick it when: One person owns the data, a handful of sites, no external audit of the figures, and the questions are the same each year.

Think again when: Several people edit the same file, a customer or verifier asks how a figure was made, or suppliers send data by email.

ESG software platform

Pick it when: Your reporting follows the frameworks the platform already supports, and you want a product with a roadmap, not a project.

Think again when: Your calculations, supplier tiers or approval steps don't fit its forms, or per-seat pricing grows faster than your use.

Consultancy

Pick it when: You need judgement: materiality, method choices, targets, or a one-off report written by experts.

Think again when: You need the same data collected and checked every cycle. Advice doesn't leave a system behind.

A custom system (Climoz)

Pick it when: Your process, calculations or suppliers don't fit a product, you already use or are willing to use Zoho, and you want to own the evidence trail.

Think again when: See below. We would rather tell you now than half-way through a build.

Honest boundaries

When not to choose Climoz

A custom system is a project. If any of these apply, another option will likely serve you better.

  • You need a rating, label or assurance opinion. We build the data system. Raters, verifiers and auditors give the opinion.
  • One standard framework covers everything you report. A platform built for that framework will be quicker to start.
  • You won't use Zoho. Our systems are built on Zoho Creator, Analytics and CRM. If that's ruled out, we're the wrong fit.
  • Nobody on your side will own it. Every system needs a named owner who answers questions during the build and runs it afterwards.
  • You need it filed next week. Use your consultant or spreadsheets for this cycle, and plan a system for the next one.

Often the answer is a mix

Most of the teams we talk to already use two of these. A consultant sets the method, a system collects and checks the data, and a rater or platform receives the answers.

Questions

Common questions

Is a custom system always cheaper than an ESG platform?

No. It depends on your number of users, how unusual your calculations are and how long you will run it. Try the cost calculator with your own quotes.

Can we use an ESG platform and Climoz together?

Yes. Some teams keep a platform for disclosure and use a Climoz system for supplier collection or an unusual calculation, then pass the results across.

Do we still need a consultant if we have a system?

Often, yes. A consultant advises on method, materiality and targets. The system holds the data and produces the figures they rely on.

What if we outgrow spreadsheets later?

Keep your spreadsheets tidy: one row per record, a source column and a factor table with years. That makes any later move faster, whichever option you choose.
